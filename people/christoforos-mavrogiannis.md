### Bio

Christoforos Mavrogiannis is an Assistant Professor of Robotics at the University of Michigan, where he runs the Fluent Robotics Lab. His mission is to enable robots to work with and around people in dynamic and unstructured environments like the home, the workspace, and public spaces. He has been recognized as an outstanding young scientist by the Heidelberg Laureate Forum, the HRI Pioneers, RSS Pioneers, and the AAAI New Faculty Highlights programs. For his open-source work with OpenBionics, he has been been a finalist for the Hackaday Prize and a winner of the Robotdalen International Innovation Award. Before joining Michigan, Christoforos completed postdoctoral training at the University of Washington. He earned his PhD from Cornell University and his Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens.
