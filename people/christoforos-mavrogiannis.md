### Bio

Christoforos Mavrogiannis is an Assistant Professor of Robotics at the University of Michigan, where he directs the Fluent Robotics Lab. Before joining Michigan in 2023, he was a postdoctoral research associate at the University of Washington's Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering, working with Siddhartha Srinivasa in the Personal Robotics Lab. He received his Ph.D. in 2019 and his M.S. in 2017 from the Sibley School of Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering at Cornell University, where he was advised by Ross Knepper, and his Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens in 2013. In 2026 he spent a semester at ETH Zürich as an academic guest of Stelian Coros's Computational Robotics Lab, supported by an ETH RobotX Robotics Research Fellowship. He was selected for the AAAI New Faculty Highlights program in 2024 and as a young researcher at the Heidelberg Laureate Forum in 2022, and he is a cofounder of OpenBionics, an open-source initiative for affordable robot and prosthetic hands.

### Research interests

His research aims to enable robots to fluently work with and around people in dynamic, unstructured environments. His interests span human-robot interaction, shared autonomy, multiagent and multirobot systems, navigation in dynamic environments, decentralized control, behavior prediction, and robotic manipulation. With the Fluent Robotics Lab, he develops algorithms, systems, and interfaces for social robot navigation among people, nonprehensile rearrangement with mobile robot pushers, and human-robot collaboration through implicit communication.
