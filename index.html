    Welcome to the Fluent Robotics Lab! Our mission is to enable robots to fluently work with and around people in dynamic, unstructured environments. To get there, we develop techniques for human behavior prediction, robot learning, and motion planning and control, and we integrate them into end-to-end autonomous systems. We validate our work through extensive experiments with real robots and users under realistic conditions, from mobile robots navigating dense crowds to robots collaborating with people and with each other.

    The lab is led by Prof. Christoforos Mavrogiannis in the Department of Robotics at the University of Michigan.
